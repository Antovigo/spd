A site is the set of readers (q, k, v at an attention input; gate, up at an MLP input) of one block that are causally important somewhere at t. Points: the alive-only model's residual stream at the site divided by its RMS, x / rho, one per domain row (distinct input at t), projected on three orthonormal axes in the readers' span; reader r's inner activation is (x / rho) . V~_r, V~_r its gain-folded unit read direction, and its arrow is V~_r projected on the same axes.
Quantity map of the selected position: rows are quantities, columns the read sites (attention input, MLP input of each block); colour = drop-one excess (share of the readers' read variance a quantity explains beyond the other accepted quantities and beyond chance, log scale 1e-4..1); white dot = dependence > 0.9 (reproduced by the other accepted quantities); red cross = stability < 0.5 (selected in fewer than half of the nested runs).
