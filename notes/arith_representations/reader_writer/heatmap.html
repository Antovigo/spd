Reader–writer heatmaps

One MLP block at a time: each cell shows how much writer w's activation depends on reader r. Rows and columns are co-clustered so that connected components sit together near the diagonal. Click a cell to open that writer in the per-layer reader/writer applet.

≥ 1 Top strip: gate reader up reader offset reader (dot) Rows: writers. Columns: readers.
Definitions

The block is MLP layer l (integer 0–31) of the decomposed Llama model (run p-ba8b0c08, step 40000, CI filter addsub-08-last-pos). Prompts are "a op b =" with integer operands a, b in 1–400 and op ∈ {+, −}; the analysis position t is the token "=" (t = 4) or "b" (t = 3).

A reader r is a gate or up component; its masked activation s_r (real number) is its inner activation times its 0/1 mask. A writer w is a down component; y_w (real number) is its inner activation, computed exactly from the readers as y_w = V_w · (silu(G) ⊙ H), where G and H (vectors of length 14336) are the sums of s_r U_r over gate and over up readers, U_r the reader's output vector and V_w the writer's input vector.

Mean ablation sets s_r to its mean over the prompts where w is on and keeps every other reader; the cell value is the resulting mean squared change of y_w divided by Var(y_w), both over those prompts. Zero ablation sets s_r to 0 instead and divides by E[y_w²]. Both are measured on the fit half of the prompt pool. Values are shown on a log scale from 10−4 to 1; cells below 10−4 show the surface colour.

An offset reader of w has mean-ablation value below 1 % and zero-ablation value above 10 %: w needs it switched on, but its variation does not matter.

Shown writers are those on in at least 50 held-out prompts (the others have no held-out R²). Shown readers have a mean-ablation value of at least 10−3 for some shown writer, or are an offset reader of one. p_on is the share of prompts in which w is on; k99 is the smallest number of top-ranked readers whose held-out R² (fraction of Var(y_w) explained with all other readers at their means) reaches 0.99, 0 when never reached.

Co-clustered order: bipartite spectral co-clustering. With A the mean-ablation matrix clipped to [0, 1], writers and readers are embedded together by singular vectors 2 to n+1 of D_w^−½ A D_r^−½ (D_w, D_r diagonal matrices of the row and column sums of A), then ordered by Ward clustering with optimal leaf ordering. "Diagonal mass" is the share of the sum of A lying within 10 % of the axis length of the diagonal; the number of singular vectors n (integer) is chosen among 4, 8 and 16 to maximise it, and "random order" is the same share after a random permutation of rows and columns.
