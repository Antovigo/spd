Reader–writer explorer

Definitions
a, b
the integer operands of the prompt "a op b =", each in 1..400; op is + or −.
t
token position (integer): 3 is the "b" token, 4 is the "=" token. Everything on the page is at the selected t.
reader r
a gate_proj or up_proj component of the block, labelled kind + component index (e.g. gate123).
writer w
a down_proj component of the block (e.g. down7).
m_r
mask of reader r on a prompt, 0 or 1: 1 when its output CI on the original model exceeds 0.01 (the CI filter's rounded-CI decomposed model).
s_r
reader r's masked inner activation, s_r = m_r · (x · V_r), real; x is the RMSNorm'd residual stream (vector, length 4096), V_r the component's read vector.
U_r
reader r's write vector into the block's d_mlp = 14336 pre-activations.
G, H
gate and up pre-activations, vectors of length 14336: G = Σ over gate readers of s_r U_r, H = the same over up readers.
V_w
writer w's read vector, length 14336.
y_w
writer w's inner activation, y_w = V_w · (silu(G) ⊙ H), real; ⊙ is the element-wise product. Grids show y_w where w is on.
s̄_r
mean of s_r over the prompts where w is on (fit half of the prompts); depends on w.
Δ_r(w)
change of y_w when s_r alone is replaced by s̄_r (mean ablation), computed exactly.
share
E[Δ_r(w)²] / Σ_r' E[Δ_r'(w)²], E the average over the fit half's prompts where w is on. Readers are ranked by it.
offset reader
reader r of writer w with E[Δ_r(w)²] < 1 % of Var(y_w) (its variation does not matter) but E[(y_w with s_r = 0 − y_w)²] > 10 % of E[y_w²] (switching it off does). "off-cost" = that last ratio.
k
number of top-ranked readers kept (integer); the others are held at their mean s̄_r over prompts where w is on.
R²_k
1 − E[(y_w − ŷ_w)²] / Var(y_w) on held-out prompts where w is on, ŷ_w the prediction with k readers.
support
the writer's top k readers, k the smallest value reaching R²_k ≥ 0.9 (or 0.99, toggle). "not reached" marks writers where the target is never met; their support is the k of best R².
a_c
component c's full inner activation x · V_c (real), before the mask: defined on every prompt, also where c is off. "Grid values: full inner a" shows it; "masked s" shows s = m · a and hatches the prompts where c is off.
mean CI
the component's output causal importance (CI, in [0, 1]) from the CI filter, averaged over the 320,000 prompts at position t. The browse list is sorted by it.
on
fraction of the 320,000 prompts (both ops) on which the component is on at t.
write share
writer's share of Σ_w P(on) · E[y_w² | on] · ‖U_w‖², U_w its write vector into the residual stream.
Grids
rows: a = 1 (top) to 400 (bottom); columns: b = 1 (left) to 400 (right); one pixel per prompt at 400 px.
Grid colours
blue negative, grey 0, red positive, symmetric scale per grid (±max|value|); hatched cells (masked view only): component off.
Pair plot
"show" picks predictor lines, prompts, or both. Lines: the predicted y_w (the formula above) as s_r is swept over the union of the ranges of s_r and a_r on the prompts where w is on; one line per value of a second support reader o, set to its 10/30/50/70/90 % quantiles over those prompts (of s_o, or of a_o when "Grid values" is full a); every other reader at its mean s̄. Points: 4000 random prompts out of all 320,000, x = s_r (or a_r), y = the writer's masked activation (or its full inner activation a_w), colour = s_o (or a_o), or the per-prompt output CI (in [0, 1]) of the plotted reader or writer, available for components with mean CI ≥ 0.05; filled squares: w on, hollow squares: w off. In the masked view an off component has s = 0 and is plotted there. The lines are the predictor of y_w and only apply where w is on. Colour is viridis over o's range on those prompts, for lines and points alike. Point values come from the grids (8-bit, 255 levels across each grid's range). The ring marks the hovered grid cell's prompt, on every prompt.
